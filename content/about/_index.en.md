I write about software, side projects, and things I’m figuring out along the way.

I work mostly on backend development, using Python to build APIs, handle data, and make systems actually run. Recently, I’ve been spending more time exploring AI and trying to apply it to real use cases, especially through my own projects.

My background isn’t in computer science—I studied finance. I got into programming because I wanted to build things for myself, and over time it just became what I do.

Outside of work, I like surfing, playing tennis, and being outdoors when the weather isn’t too hot. I’m also trying to move toward a slower and more sustainable way of living.

This blog is just a place where I keep notes—things I build, things I learn, and sometimes just random thoughts.
